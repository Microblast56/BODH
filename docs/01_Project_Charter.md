\# Project Name



BODH



\---



\# Tagline



> From Data to Decisions.



\---



\# Vision



To empower businesses of every size to make confident, intelligent, and data-driven decisions by transforming raw business data into actionable knowledge using Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.



\---



\# Mission



To build an AI-powered Decision Intelligence Platform capable of collecting, analyzing, predicting, and explaining business data, enabling organizations to take better decisions instead of simply viewing reports.



\---



\# Problem Statement



Modern businesses generate enormous amounts of operational data every day. While dashboards and reporting tools explain what has already happened, they rarely recommend what should happen next.



BODH bridges this gap by combining Data Analytics, Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, and Business Intelligence to provide actionable recommendations, predictive insights, and explainable decision support.



\---



\# Objectives



\- Build a production-ready AI application.

\- Learn industry-standard software engineering practices.

\- Gain expertise in Data Science, Data Engineering, Machine Learning, MLOps, and Cloud Deployment.

\- Create a product suitable for real-world deployment.

\- Build a portfolio-quality flagship project.
